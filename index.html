What exactly will I receive?+

A 30-page digital PDF in American English, with illustrated explanations, age-organized chapters, two observation worksheets with six fillable fields, appointment preparation, and 15 linked references and resources. No printed book or app is included.

What ages does the guide cover?+

The guide is for parents of children ages 1–10. Its chapters cover 12–36 months, ages 2–5, and ages 6–10. The first two chapters overlap at 24–36 months, so you can read either or both.

Can I read it on my phone?+

Yes. The portrait format and large text are designed for mobile reading. You can also open the PDF on a tablet or computer. Typing in the worksheet fields requires a PDF reader that supports fillable forms; you can also copy the prompts into your own notes.

Will this tell me whether my child is autistic?+

No. AutiSpark cannot confirm or rule out autism. It is an educational guide, not a diagnostic test or a validated screening tool. It helps you organize observations and prepare questions. A qualified professional can evaluate your child’s development.
